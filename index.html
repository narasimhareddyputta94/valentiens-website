My Dearest Love,

As I sit here writing this, my heart overflows with emotions that words can barely capture. You came into my life like a beautiful dream I never want to wake up from.

You've taught me that love isn't just a feeling — it's a choice we make every day. And every day, I choose you. I choose your imperfections, your quirks, your dreams, and your fears. I choose all of you.

On this Valentine's Day, I want you to know: you are my person. The one my heart recognizes. The one my soul has been searching for across lifetimes.

I promise to love you on the easy days and the hard days. When the world is loud, I'll be your quiet place. When life gets heavy, I'll help you carry the weight.

You are loved. You are cherished. You are my forever.

With all my heart and soul,

Your Valentine 💕
